Privacy Policy

Coach Connect · Last updated: August 6, 2026

Plain-language summary of how Coach Connect handles personal information. This is not personal legal advice.

1. Who we are

Coach Connect is operated from the United States. The app may be available in other countries through the app stores. If something here conflicts with what a qualified lawyer tells you, follow your lawyer.

2. What we collect

Account and profile information you provide (such as name, email, role as client or trainer, and profile fields you choose to fill in). Fitness-related content you enter or upload, such as workouts, nutrition logs, progress metrics, photos, notes, and files. Messages and other content shared in the product. Preferences such as theme and whether optional AI features are on. Technical data needed to run the app, including device push tokens for notifications you allow, authentication identifiers, and diagnostic information when something fails.

3. How and why we use information

We use personal information to provide the service you asked for (accounts, dashboards, messaging, file sharing, reminders, and optional AI coaching when you turn it on), to secure accounts, to fix bugs, to respond to support requests, and to comply with law where required.

4. Health and fitness-related information

Coach Connect is built for coaching and fitness workflows. Information you add can be sensitive (for example weight, injuries, goals, or meal details). Do not use the app as a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

5. Coaches and clients

If you connect with a trainer or client inside the product, information the app is designed to share with that person (such as progress, messages, shared files, and similar) may be visible to them as part of using Coach Connect.

6. Service providers

We rely on third-party infrastructure including Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions) and notification services for push messages. Payment processors such as Apple (In-App Purchases) and Stripe (client coaching payments) may process payment-related data when you use those features.

7. International use

Your information may be stored or processed in the United States or in other regions where our providers operate. Laws such as the GDPR may give you additional rights depending on your situation.

8. Subscriptions and payments

If you subscribe to Coach Connect Pro as a trainer, Apple processes payment through your Apple ID. We receive subscription status from Apple to unlock trainer features. We do not receive your full payment card number from Apple. Client-to-trainer coaching payments may be processed by Stripe when that feature is enabled.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information. You can delete your account from in-app Settings. For other requests, email coachconnect0@gmail.com or use Contact Support in Settings.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

11. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to operate Coach Connect, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations.

12. Children

Coach Connect is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: coachconnect0@gmail.com
